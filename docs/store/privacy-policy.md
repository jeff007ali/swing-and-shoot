# Swing and Shoot: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

Swing and Shoot does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

**What stays on your device**
- Your settings (sound, haptics, button layout, colors) and your local profile: a random player ID made on
  first launch, the display name you type for LAN games, and simple stats such as matches played and won.
- A list of your last 50 match results (mode, arena, scores, duration).
- Optional playtest logs (gameplay numbers only), if turned on in Settings.

None of this leaves your device unless you copy it off yourself.

**Local network (LAN play)**
- LAN Party connects phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot directly to each other. To find games it sends a small
  announcement on the local network (game name, player count, mode and arena). Your display name is shown to the
  other players in the same lobby. Nothing is sent to the internet or to us.
- Android asks for network access and Wi-Fi multicast for this; iOS asks for local network access.

**No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The game has no in-app purchases in this version.

**Children:** the game collects no data from anyone, including children.
